TinyMind Privacy Policy

What we collect

TinyMind creates an anonymous Firebase Authentication account on this device by default. Email backup is optional; if a parent links an email and password, Firebase processes those details, but TinyMind does not store the password itself.

For each child profile, we store the child’s nickname, avatar, age, points, answer statistics, activity stars and daily learning records in Cloud Firestore under the current account.

On the device, SharedPreferences stores the music mute and color-friendly mode settings, the parent PIN record, and a local cache of learning progress. The PIN is required every time the parent area is opened and is not synced.

What we do NOT collect

TinyMind does not collect location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings or advertising IDs. The app does not use advertising or third-party analytics.

How we use it

We use this information only to save and show learning progress to the parent.

Who can see it

Only the holder of the current Firebase account can access that account’s child profiles and learning data. Google Firebase stores and processes the data on our behalf as a data processor. We do not sell or share personal data with others.

Data is stored in Google Firebase’s Singapore region (asia-southeast1).

TinyMind never receives or stores your fingerprint or face data; your phone's own security handles it. If you choose a PIN, it is kept only on this device in a scrambled form.

Children’s privacy

TinyMind is designed for children aged 3–6 to use at home with a parent’s supervision. The app starts with an anonymous account so children can reach the child picker without an email login. The parent area requires the device PIN on every entry.

Your choices

Parents can optionally link an email as a backup while keeping the same account. Without an email backup, data is lost if the app is uninstalled or the device is changed. Signing in to an existing backup shows that account’s children. If children are saved on the phone, the parent can choose to copy them into the account as new children. The previous anonymous record stays unreachable and may remain stored until it is cleaned up.

Parents can delete a child profile at any time from the parent area. Deleting the parent account also deletes its child profiles and learning data.

Parents can export a learning report from the parent area. The report is generated on the phone, and parents decide whom to share it with. TinyMind does not send it automatically.
