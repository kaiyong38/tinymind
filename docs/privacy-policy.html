TinyMind Privacy Policy

What we collect

For a parent account, Firebase Authentication processes the parent’s email address and password. TinyMind does not store the password itself.

For each child profile, we store the child’s nickname, avatar, age, points, answer statistics, activity stars and daily learning records in Cloud Firestore under the parent’s account.

On the device, SharedPreferences stores the music mute and color-friendly mode settings, and the parent’s email if they choose to remember it on that device. A local cache keeps the selected child’s learning progress available offline.

What we do NOT collect

TinyMind does not collect location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings or advertising IDs. The app does not use advertising or third-party analytics.

How we use it

We use this information only to save and show learning progress to the parent.

Who can see it

Only the parent signed in to the account can access that account’s child profiles and learning data. Google Firebase stores and processes the data on our behalf as a data processor. We do not sell or share personal data with others.

TinyMind never receives or stores your fingerprint or face data; your phone's own security handles it. If you choose a PIN, it is kept only on this device in a scrambled form.

Children’s privacy

TinyMind is designed for children aged 3–6 to use at home with a parent’s supervision. A parent creates and manages the account.

Your choices

Parents can delete a child profile at any time from the parent area. Deleting the parent account also deletes its child profiles and learning data.
